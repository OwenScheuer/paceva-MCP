# What an AI app can see

When you connect Paceva to Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client, that app can
read, for **your account only**:

- workouts: sport, time, distance, pace/speed/power, heart rate, cadence, laps
- wellness: HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, recovery / Body Battery
- fitness, fatigue and form; race predictions and race plans; your race bike

It **cannot** change anything, see other people's data, see your email or
password, or reach your Intervals.icu or watch accounts directly.

What the AI app does with the answers is governed by **that app's** privacy
policy (Anthropic's, OpenAI's, …), not Paceva's. Paceva never sends your data
to an AI app you haven't connected yourself.

Every value carries its source:

| `source` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `device` | Straight from your watch (via Intervals.icu or Apple Health) |
| `paceva` | Computed by Paceva from your sessions |

Full policy: [paceva.app/privacy](https://paceva.app/privacy).

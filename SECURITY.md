# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately to **privacy@paceva.app** with steps
to reproduce. Don't open a public issue or test against other people's
accounts. We'll acknowledge within a few days and keep you posted while we fix
it.

In scope: `paceva.app`, the MCP endpoint, the OAuth server, and the
Intervals.icu connection. Out of scope: Intervals.icu, watch makers and AI
apps themselves (report to them), and volume/denial-of-service testing.
